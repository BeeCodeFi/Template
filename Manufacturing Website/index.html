About Forge

Built for
Precision

Since 1998, Forge Industries has been at the forefront of advanced manufacturing. Our 120,000 sq ft state-of-the-art facility houses over 200 CNC machines, robotic cells, and dedicated quality labs.

We serve the world's most demanding industries — aerospace, defence, medical, and automotive — delivering components with tolerances measured in microns and quality measured in zero defects.

How We Work

Our Manufacturing
Process

From initial consultation to final delivery — a streamlined, transparent workflow engineered for efficiency.

Step 01

Design & Engineering

DFM analysis, CAD/CAM programming, material selection, and tolerance optimization using Siemens NX and SolidWorks.

Step 02

Prototyping

Rapid prototyping via 5-axis CNC and metal additive manufacturing. First articles in as few as 5 business days.

Step 03

Production

Automated production cells with in-line SPC monitoring, robotic material handling, and lights-out manufacturing capability.

Step 04

Delivery & Support

Full traceability, AS9102 FAIR documentation, and global logistics partnerships. 98.7% on-time delivery rate.
