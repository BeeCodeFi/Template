Our Story

Where Ancient Spice
Meets Modern Artistry

Rasa was born from a single idea: to honour the soul of Indian cuisine without being bound by its traditions. Chef Arjun Mehta left Mumbai's bustling kitchens to Ranchi with a suitcase of spice blends and a mission — to create food that feels like memory.

Every dish at Rasa tells a story — of coast and mountain, of grandmother's recipe and molecular precision, of fire-roasted char and the delicate perfume of rose water. We source our spices directly from Kerala and Rajasthan, our herbs from the local Jharkhand farms.
